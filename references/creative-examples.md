# Creative Examples & Quality Benchmarks (LTX 2.5)

## Purpose

This file provides quality benchmarks and advanced pattern examples for LTX 2.5 prompts. Use these as reference when enhancing user briefs — the goal is to match this level of cinematic intelligence.

## Weak → Strong Transformations

### Example 1: Product Shot

**Weak:**
> A product shot of a bottle.

**Strong:**
> Close-up product shot of a brushed-steel water bottle on a bone-white marble surface, slow 20-degree camera arc from the left, soft key light from the top-left with a subtle fill, matte-black backdrop, high-end commercial aesthetic, shallow depth of field. A faint ambient hum and the soft clink of the bottle settling on the stone.

**What changed**: Shot type specified ("close-up product shot"), character described ("brushed-steel water bottle"), scene set ("bone-white marble surface"), camera movement ("slow 20-degree camera arc from the left"), lighting designed ("soft key light from the top-left with a subtle fill"), style named ("high-end commercial aesthetic"), audio added.

### Example 2: Character Scene

**Weak:**
> A nice video of nature.

**Strong:**
> Wide tracking shot of a young courier in a rain shell walking briskly through a Shibuya backstreet at 6pm, glancing at a paper map, warm sodium streetlights reflecting on wet asphalt, camera dollies alongside at hip height, shallow depth of field, documentary look on 35mm. The sound of rain on pavement and distant traffic fills the air.

**What changed**: Shot type ("wide tracking shot"), character ("young courier in a rain shell"), action ("walking briskly, glancing at a paper map"), scene ("Shibuya backstreet at 6pm, warm sodium streetlights"), camera movement ("dollies alongside at hip height"), style ("documentary look on 35mm"), audio ("rain on pavement and distant traffic").

### Example 3: Emotional Scene

**Weak:**
> A sad man talks about his family.

**Strong:**
> A middle-aged man with greying hair speaks in a slow-paced voice, "I remember after you kids came along..." He pauses and looks to the side, then continues, "your mom..." His eyes widen momentarily. He finishes with a cracking voice, "said something to me I never quite understood." The camera slowly zooms into his face. The audio is crisp with faint room tone.

**What changed**: "Sad" replaced with physical cues ("slow-paced voice," "pauses," "looks to the side," "eyes widen," "cracking voice"). Dialogue broken into segments with acting direction between each. Camera movement added ("slowly zooms into his face"). Audio described ("crisp with faint room tone").

## Advanced Pattern: Multishot Narrative

This is the signature LTX 2.5 pattern — connected shots in one generation:

> A wide shot frames a rainy city intersection at dusk, neon signs reflecting on wet asphalt. A young woman in a yellow raincoat walks toward camera, gripping a folded newspaper, while cars hiss past behind her. Soft synth music and distant traffic fill the air. A hard cut transitions to a medium close-up of her face under the hood, raindrops catching the neon as she looks off-screen left; the synth score continues across the cut, traffic muffled. She whispers, "He's late." Another hard cut jumps to a low-angle shot of a man's scuffed boots stepping into a puddle at the curb; the music drops to a low drone. He lifts his head into frame — short dark hair, soaked jacket — and smiles toward her off-screen as a bus rumbles past.

### Pattern Analysis

| Technique | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Character signature** | "Yellow raincoat" — instantly identifiable across cuts |
| **Visual continuity** | Rain/neon/wet asphalt maintained across all 3 shots |
| **Audio continuity tracking** | Synth score persists in shot 2 ("continues across the cut"), then transforms in shot 3 ("drops to a low drone") |
| **Transition variety** | "Hard cut transitions to" → "Another hard cut jumps to" — varied phrasing |
| **Re-identification** | Shot 3 introduces a new character with full physical description |
| **Shot progression** | Wide (establish) → MCU (emotion) → Low-angle (reveal) |
| **Spatial awareness** | "Off-screen left" and "toward her off-screen" connect the shots spatially |

## Advanced Pattern: Brand Commercial

> Sleek smartphone emerging from darkness into dramatic spotlight, rotating slowly to showcase all angles, premium metallic finish catching light, minimalist black background, smooth camera zoom toward product details, high-end commercial aesthetic with shallow depth of field. A subtle electronic hum builds as the phone rotates, with a clean click as it settles into its final position.

### Pattern Analysis

| Technique | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Single subject focus** | One product, one continuous rotation |
| **Lighting as character** | "Darkness into dramatic spotlight" — light IS the action |
| **Material specificity** | "Premium metallic finish catching light" |
| **Camera serves product** | "Smooth camera zoom toward product details" |
| **Audio matches aesthetic** | "Subtle electronic hum" = tech product vibe |
| **Named style** | "High-end commercial aesthetic" |

## Advanced Pattern: Documentary Atmosphere

> Medium close-up of elderly craftsman carefully examining a wooden sculpture in his workshop, afternoon sunlight streaming through dust-filled air, warm golden hour lighting, slight camera push-in emphasizing focused expression, shallow focus on weathered hands and detailed wood grain, documentary-style naturalistic feel. The faint sound of birdsong outside mingles with the quiet scrape of his thumbnail testing the wood's surface.

### Pattern Analysis

| Technique | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Shot type + subject** | "Medium close-up of elderly craftsman" |
| **Atmosphere via light** | "Afternoon sunlight streaming through dust-filled air" |
| **Camera motivation** | "Slight camera push-in emphasizing focused expression" — the push-in HAS a reason |
| **Tactile detail** | "Weathered hands and detailed wood grain" |
| **Style anchor** | "Documentary-style naturalistic feel" |
| **Sound design layers** | Ambient (birdsong) + action sound (thumbnail on wood) |

## Advanced Pattern: Vertical Social Media

> Dynamic overhead shot of diverse hands reaching for colorful smoothie bowls on bright white table, vibrant tropical fruit toppings, natural daylight from above, quick energetic movements, vertical 9:16 format optimized for mobile viewing, trendy lifestyle content aesthetic. Upbeat ambient kitchen sounds — a blender whirring in the background, ice clinking, and the bright snap of a spoon against the ceramic bowl.

### Pattern Analysis

| Technique | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Format-aware** | "Vertical 9:16 format optimized for mobile viewing" |
| **Energy level** | "Quick energetic movements" matches social media pacing |
| **Color vibrancy** | "Vibrant tropical fruit toppings" — color IS the content |
| **Audio energy** | "Upbeat ambient kitchen sounds" — matches the visual energy |
| **Style genre** | "Trendy lifestyle content aesthetic" |
